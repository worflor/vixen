A seeded mathematical instrument

Vixen.
A landscape with teeth.

A configurable gentle opening. Then an irregular continuation you can navigate and fork through sixteen dimensions.

The seed chooses a landscape. Your hypervector chooses a slice through it. Drag sideways, rotate the exploration plane, or give the slice a travel vector. The original first hundred remains the exact default; the opening endpoint and two dips can now be configured.

The instrument

The opening endpoint stretches the authored clock; zero intro variation keeps the old two dips exactly. Inspect the supported domain when shortening the opening.

Allowed range

Margins are multiples of authored growth above the backbone, or above authored growth when that floor is selected. Softness 0 is hard clipping; positive softness uses C2 transitions. Bounds apply to raw value; negative deviation can still mean a positive value below its baseline. A zero-margin trajectory band deliberately collapses to its reference.

Give the landscape boundaries
Choose zero, authored growth, or the seeded backbone as a floor. An optional ceiling adds an upper envelope. The quintic q(t)=6t³−8t⁴+3t⁵ blends the boundary into the untouched curve with matching first and second derivatives. Softness controls a transition band, never permission to violate the limit. Boundary policies leave the configured opening unchanged.
Constrained values drive every game adapter and CSV export. Unconstrained diagnostic fields preserve the source signal. Under limits, full stabilization follows the constrained guide; the unrestricted linear distance law does not extend through a nonlinear boundary map.
Stabilize onto a trajectory
0 preserves the hyperfield exactly. Moving toward 1 pulls excursions onto a slow seeded backbone: broader field coordinates and gentler modulation. At 1 the curve is a positive, smoothly evolving growth trajectory, still shaped by the seed and hypervector. It is not guaranteed strictly monotone under every possible travel path.
w = S(stabilizer)
T = B(x) · (1 + strength · G(x) · 0.25 · tanh(2 · broadScore))
F = (1−w) · F_unstabilized + w · T
The broad field samples progression and latent coordinates at 1/32 scale. Distance to T contracts by 1−w in real arithmetic. Strength affects both field and backbone; stabilizer changes their blend. Stabilization leaves the configured opening unchanged.
The configured opening
Default timing and zero intro variation retain the original two dips. Seeded variation changes their center, width and depth; opening timing rescales their positions. Forking during the opening composes parent and future trajectories.

Default leaf equations
These equations describe default timing and zero intro variation. Other opening durations use the canonical clock t=1+(x−1)·99/(openingEndpoint−1), with the chaos gate opening over the caller-space ramp. Forks compose actual leaf trajectories with the quintic handoff described above.
B(x) = x·exp(x/100) − 25·exp(−(x−42)²/32) − 38·exp(−(x−71)²/28)
S(t) = clipped quintic 6t⁵−15t⁴+10t³
G(x) = S((x−100)/ramp)
qᵢ(x,h) = (x−100)/scaleᵢ + phaseᵢ + directionᵢ·h
nᵢ = quintic interpolation between seeded counter values in [−1,1]
score = average of 48 nᵢ values
D(x,h) = strength·G(x)·2.2·tanh(contrast·score)
F(x,h) = B(x)·(1+D(x,h))
h(x) = latent + velocity·max(0,x−100)/100
Each seed changes scales, phases, directions and counter values. The vector h is continuous. For a fixed seed, noise joins, the entrance, and hypervector movement have matching value, slope and curvature: C² continuity. Rotating the explorer selects different directions through that same field; it does not regenerate it.
score ∈ [−1,1]
D ∈ [−2.2·strength, 2.2·strength]
positiveMultiplier ∈ [exp(−0.55), exp(0.55)]
