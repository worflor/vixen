Vixen.
A landscape with teeth.

One gentle century. Then an irregular continuation you can navigate through sixteen dimensions.

The seed chooses a landscape. Your hypervector chooses a slice through it. Drag sideways, rotate the exploration plane, or give the slice a travel vector. The first hundred stays exactly where it was.

Stabilize onto a trajectory
0 preserves the hyperfield exactly. Moving toward 1 pulls excursions onto a slow seeded backbone: broader field coordinates and gentler modulation. At 1 the curve is a positive, smoothly evolving growth trajectory, still shaped by the seed and hypervector. It is not guaranteed strictly monotone under every possible travel path.
w = S(stabilizer)
T = B(x) · (1 + strength · G(x) · 0.25 · tanh(2 · broadScore))
F = (1−w) · F_unstabilized + w · T
The broad field samples progression and latent coordinates at 1/32 scale. Distance to T contracts by 1−w in real arithmetic. Strength affects both field and backbone; stabilizer changes their blend. All first-hundred values remain untouched.

Choose the output your game needs
  • Raw signed value: growth with genuine negative excursions.
  • Signed deviation / multiplier: modulation around an authored baseline.
  • Positive multiplier: smooth exp(0.55·tanh(D)), bounded by exp(±0.55), neutral at D=0.
  • Activity: a 0–1 control signal, not a probability or a random draw.

The mathematical object
B(x) = x·exp(x/100) − 25·exp(−(x−42)²/32) − 38·exp(−(x−71)²/28)
S(t) = clipped quintic 6t⁵−15t⁴+10t³
G(x) = S((x−100)/ramp)
qᵢ(x,h) = (x−100)/scaleᵢ + phaseᵢ + directionᵢ·h
nᵢ = quintic interpolation between seeded counter values in [−1,1]
score = average of 48 nᵢ values
D(x,h) = strength·G(x)·2.2·tanh(contrast·score)
F(x,h) = B(x)·(1+D(x,h))
h(x) = latent + velocity·max(0,x−100)/100
Each seed changes scales, phases, directions and counter values. The vector h is continuous. For a fixed seed, noise joins, the entrance, and hypervector movement have matching value, slope and curvature: C² continuity. Rotating the explorer selects different directions through that same field; it does not regenerate it.
score ∈ [−1,1]
D ∈ [−2.2·strength, 2.2·strength]
positiveMultiplier ∈ [exp(−0.55), exp(0.55)]
First-hundred samples are exactly the original baseline.
O(48) work per configured-path sample; no prior samples needed.
